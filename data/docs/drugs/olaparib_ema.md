# Olaparib (Lynparza) - EMA Regulatory Summary

## 1. Regulatory Status & Approval Pathway
- **Drug Name:** Lynparza (olaparib)
- **Jurisdiction:** European Union (European Medicines Agency - EMA / European Commission)
- **Approval Date for Combination mCRPC:** December 21, 2022 (CHMP positive opinion adopted November 2022)
- **Approved Regulatory Pathway:** Centralised Marketing Authorisation for broad mCRPC patient population without mandatory BRCA mutation restriction.

## 2. Therapeutic Indications & Patient Population
- **SmPC Section 4.1 Approved Indication:** Lynparza in combination with abiraterone and prednisone or prednisolone is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with **metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) in whom chemotherapy is not clinically indicated**.
- **Broad European Scope:** Unlike the US FDA (which restricted the indication to BRCA-mutated patients only), the EMA approved the combination for the broader mCRPC indication without requiring BRCA mutation testing as an obligatory eligibility criteria.

## 3. Clinical Trial Evidence & Endpoints
- **PROpel Phase 3 Trial Evaluation:** EMA/CHMP reviewed the 796-patient PROpel trial, emphasizing the statistically significant prolongation of radiographic progression-free survival (rPFS) in the overall intention-to-treat (ITT) population:
  - Median rPFS by investigator: 24.8 months for olaparib + abiraterone vs. 16.6 months for placebo + abiraterone (Hazard Ratio: 0.66, 95% CI: 0.54 to 0.81, p < 0.0001).
- **EMA Scientific Rationale for Broad Approval vs. FDA Restriction:**
  - The CHMP acknowledged that the magnitude of treatment effect was largest in the BRCA-mutated subgroup.
  - However, the CHMP determined that the statistically robust 8.2-month median rPFS improvement observed in the overall population provided clinically meaningful delay in disease progression for patients who had not yet received chemotherapy, justifying a broader label in Europe.

## 4. Safety Warnings & Risk Management
- **Key Warnings (SmPC Section 4.4):** Hematological toxicity (severe anemia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia), venous thromboembolism, and monitoring of complete blood counts prior to and monthly during treatment.
- **Divergence Summary vs. US FDA:** 
  - **US FDA:** Restricted approval strictly to BRCA-mutated mCRPC (determining non-BRCA patients suffer toxicity without proven survival benefit).
  - **EMA:** Approved for all mCRPC patients in whom chemotherapy is not clinically indicated, accepting the full trial ITT progression-free survival outcome.
