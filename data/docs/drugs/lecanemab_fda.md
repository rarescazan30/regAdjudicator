# Lecanemab (Leqembi) - FDA Regulatory Summary

## 1. Regulatory Status & Approval Pathway
- **Drug Name:** LEQEMBI (lecanemab-irmb) injection, for intravenous use
- **Application Type:** Biologics License Application (BLA 761269)
- **Initial Accelerated Approval:** January 6, 2023 (based on amyloid PET reduction in Phase 2 Study 201)
- **Traditional Full Approval:** July 6, 2023 (converted to traditional standard approval based on positive Phase 3 confirmatory trial data)
- **Regulatory Pathway:** Traditional Standard Approval with Boxed Warning for ARIA under 21 CFR Part 601.

## 2. Therapeutic Indications & Patient Population
- **Approved Indication:** LEQEMBI is indicated for the treatment of Alzheimer's disease.
- **Target Population:** Treatment should be initiated in patients with mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia stage of disease (early Alzheimer's disease), the population in which treatment was initiated in clinical trials.
- **Pathology Requirement:** Confirmation of the presence of amyloid-beta pathology prior to initiating treatment is required.
- **ApoE ε4 Prescription Scope:** FDA recommends testing patients for Apolipoprotein E ε4 (ApoE ε4) status prior to treatment initiation to inform discussions of ARIA risk. However, FDA did NOT restrict or contraindicate the drug in ApoE ε4 carriers or homozygotes; it is approved for the broad early Alzheimer's population regardless of genotype.

## 3. Clinical Trial Evidence & Endpoints
- **Confirmatory Phase 3 Study (Clarity AD / Study 301 / NCT03887455):** Evaluated in a double-blind, placebo-controlled, 18-month randomized trial in 1,795 patients with early Alzheimer's disease (10 mg/kg biweekly vs. placebo).
- **Primary Clinical Endpoint Met:** Met its primary endpoint with a statistically significant 27% slowing of clinical decline on the Clinical Dementia Rating-Sum of Boxes (CDR-SB) at 18 months compared to placebo (treatment difference: -0.45, 95% CI: -0.67 to -0.23, p < 0.001).
- **Key Secondary Endpoints Met:** Statistically significant improvements were observed across all key secondary clinical endpoints:
  - ADAS-Cog14 (cognitive decline slowed by 26%, p < 0.001)
  - ADCS MCI-ADL (activities of daily living decline slowed by 37%, p < 0.001)
  - Amyloid PET composite burden significantly reduced to below the threshold of amyloid positivity at 18 months.

## 4. Safety Warnings & Risk Management
- **Boxed Warning for ARIA:** Monoclonal antibodies directed against aggregated forms of beta-amyloid, including LEQEMBI, cause Amyloid Related Imaging Abnormalities (ARIA):
  - **ARIA-E:** Brain edema/effusion occurred in 12.6% of lecanemab-treated patients (vs. 1.7% placebo); symptomatic ARIA-E occurred in 2.8%.
  - **ARIA-H:** Cerebral microhemorrhages and superficial siderosis occurred in 17.3% (vs. 9.0% placebo).
- **Genetic Risk Factor (ApoE ε4):** The incidence of ARIA was substantially higher in ApoE ε4 homozygotes (32.6% ARIA-E in homozygotes vs. 10.9% in heterozygotes and 5.4% in non-carriers).
- **MRI Monitoring Protocol:** Mandatory brain MRI scans prior to initiating treatment, and before the 5th, 7th, and 14th infusions.
