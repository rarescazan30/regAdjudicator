# Olaparib (Lynparza) - FDA Regulatory Summary

## 1. Regulatory Status & Approval Pathway
- **Drug Name:** LYNPARZA (olaparib) tablets, for oral use
- **Jurisdiction:** United States (FDA - Food and Drug Administration)
- **NDA Approval for Combination Prostate Cancer:** May 31, 2023 (sNDA 208558/S-025)
- **Approved Indication Pathway:** Standard Approval with restricted population limitation (limited strictly to BRCA-mutated mCRPC).

## 2. Therapeutic Indications & Patient Population
- **Approved Indication (Prostate Cancer Combination):** LYNPARZA in combination with abiraterone and prednisone (or prednisolone) is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with deleterious or suspected deleterious **BRCA-mutated (BRCAm)** metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC).
- **Strict FDA Population Limitation:** FDA explicitly restricted the combination indication ONLY to patients with BRCA1 or BRCA2 mutations. The FDA rejected the sponsor's application to approve the combination in the broader Homologous Recombination Repair (HRR) gene-mutated or unselected mCRPC population.
- **Diagnostic Requirement:** Selection of patients requires an FDA-approved companion diagnostic (such as FoundationOne CDx) to confirm the presence of deleterious germline or somatic BRCA mutations.

## 3. Clinical Trial Evidence & Endpoints
- **Pivotal Study (PROpel / NCT03732820):** A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial in 796 patients with first-line mCRPC evaluating olaparib plus abiraterone vs. placebo plus abiraterone.
- **Primary Endpoint in Overall Population:** Radiographic Progression-Free Survival (rPFS) assessed by investigator was statistically significant in the overall trial population (HR 0.66, p < 0.001).
- **Subgroup Analysis & FDA ODAC Determination:**
  - **BRCA-Mutated Subgroup (n=85):** Showed an extraordinary clinical benefit on rPFS (Hazard Ratio: 0.24, 95% CI: 0.12 to 0.45) and a substantial trend in Overall Survival (OS Hazard Ratio: 0.30).
  - **Non-BRCA Subgroup (n=711):** The rPFS benefit was marginal and not clinically convincing (Hazard Ratio: 0.77, 95% CI: 0.63 to 0.96), with no demonstrable overall survival benefit (OS Hazard Ratio: 0.92).
  - **FDA Oncologic Drugs Advisory Committee (ODAC) Ruling:** Voted that the benefit-risk of the regimen was favourable ONLY in patients with BRCA mutations. Adding olaparib to abiraterone in non-BRCA patients caused substantial added hematological toxicity without proven clinical survival advantage.

## 4. Safety Warnings & Risk Management
- **Key Hematological Adverse Reactions:** Myelosuppression including severe anemia (Grade ≥3 in 46% of patients), neutropenia, and thrombocytopenia. 18% of patients required blood transfusions.
- **Other Toxicities:** Venous thromboembolism (pulmonary embolism in 7%), myelodysplastic syndrome/acute myeloid leukemia (MDS/AML).
- **FDA Safety Rationale:** Given the severe anemia and transfusion burdens, FDA concluded exposure to olaparib in non-BRCA patients could not be justified.
