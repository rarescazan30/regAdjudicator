# Bevacizumab (Avastin) - FDA Regulatory Summary

## 1. Regulatory Status & Approval Pathway
- **Drug Name:** AVASTIN / MVASI (bevacizumab) injection, for intravenous use
- **Jurisdiction:** United States (FDA - Food and Drug Administration)
- **Initial Accelerated Approval in Breast Cancer:** February 22, 2008 (granted accelerated approval under 21 CFR 314 Subpart H for first-line HER2-negative metastatic breast cancer in combination with paclitaxel, based on progression-free survival in trial E2100).
- **Formal Indication Revocation Date:** November 18, 2011 (FDA Commissioner Margaret Hamburg issued a formal final order withdrawing approval for the metastatic breast cancer indication under 21 CFR 314.530).
- **Current FDA Status in Breast Cancer:** REVOKED / NOT APPROVED. (Avastin remains FDA-approved for other indications including metastatic colorectal, non-small cell lung, glioblastoma, cervical, ovarian, and renal cell cancer).

## 2. Therapeutic Indications & Patient Population
- **Metastatic Breast Cancer (mBC):** NOT an approved indication in the United States. Prescribing bevacizumab for breast cancer is outside the FDA-approved labeling.
- **Other Retained FDA Indications:** Metastatic colorectal cancer (in combination with 5-FU based regimens), non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer, recurrent glioblastoma, metastatic renal cell carcinoma.

## 3. Clinical Trial Evidence & Grounds for Revocation
- **Initial Trial (E2100):** Showed an estimated 5.5-month improvement in progression-free survival (PFS) (11.8 vs. 5.9 months, HR 0.48), which supported the initial accelerated approval.
- **Confirmatory Trials (AVADO and RIBBON-1):** In the mandatory post-approval confirmatory trials, the PFS improvement was substantially smaller (ranging from only 0.9 to 2.9 months) and completely failed to demonstrate an improvement in Overall Survival (OS):
  - **AVADO (PFS gain):** ~0.9 months; Overall Survival HR: 1.03 (no survival advantage).
  - **RIBBON-1 (PFS gain):** ~2.9 months; Overall Survival HR: 1.03 (no survival advantage).
- **FDA Regulatory Conclusion:** Under the statutory Accelerated Approval framework, a surrogate or intermediate endpoint (such as PFS) must translate into verified clinical benefit. The FDA concluded that a modest, unconfirmed delay in tumor growth without overall survival prolongation or improvement in quality of life did not justify the severe treatment-related toxicities.

## 4. Safety Warnings & Risk Management
- **Severe and Life-Threatening Toxicities:** Bevacizumab demonstrated substantial systemic toxicities in clinical trials, including:
  - Gastrointestinal perforations and fistulae (often fatal).
  - Severe uncontrolled hypertension and hypertensive crises.
  - Arterial thromboembolic events (myocardial infarction, cerebral infarction).
  - Severe hemorrhage, impaired wound healing, and congestive heart failure.
- **Benefit-Risk Judgment:** FDA ruled that the risks of fatal hemorrhage and severe organ toxicity significantly outweighed the minimal progression-free survival increment in the absence of overall survival extension.
