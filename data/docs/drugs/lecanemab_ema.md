# Lecanemab (Leqembi) - EMA Regulatory Summary

## 1. Regulatory Status & Approval Pathway
- **Drug Name:** Leqembi (lecanemab)
- **Jurisdiction:** European Union (European Medicines Agency - EMA / CHMP)
- **Initial Negative Opinion:** On July 25, 2024, the CHMP initially recommended refusal of marketing authorisation, concluding that the modest cognitive benefits across the entire study population were outweighed by the risk of severe ARIA events.
- **Revised Positive Opinion Upon Re-examination:** On November 14, 2024, following an applicant re-examination and sub-population re-analysis, the CHMP recommended granting a Marketing Authorisation with **Specific Population Restrictions**.
- **Current Status in the EU:** Approved under Restricted Indication (strictly limited to ApoE ε4 non-carriers and heterozygotes).

## 2. Therapeutic Indications & Patient Population
- **Approved Indication (Restricted):** Treatment of adult patients with mild cognitive impairment due to Alzheimer's disease or mild Alzheimer's disease dementia (early Alzheimer's disease) who have **0 or 1 copy of the ApoE ε4 allele** (i.e., non-carriers or heterozygotes).
- **Explicit Exclusion / Contraindication in EU:** Leqembi is **strictly NOT recommended / excluded** in patients who are **ApoE ε4 homozygotes (2 copies of ApoE ε4 allele)**.
- **Mandatory Pre-Treatment Testing:** Pre-treatment genetic testing for ApoE ε4 genotype is mandatory in European healthcare settings before prescribing.

## 3. Clinical Trial Evidence & Endpoints
- **Phase 3 Clarity AD Trial Analysis:** CHMP evaluated the 1,795-patient Clarity AD trial, confirming the overall 27% reduction in CDR-SB decline (-0.45 difference at 18 months).
- **EMA Sub-population Rationale for Divergence:**
  - In the overall trial population, the incidence of serious and symptomatic ARIA was concerning.
  - In **ApoE ε4 homozygotes**, the risk of severe ARIA (including massive cerebral edema and macrohemorrhages) was unacceptably high (ARIA-E ~33%) and deemed to outweigh the modest clinical benefit.
  - In **non-carriers and heterozygotes (0 or 1 allele)**, the risk of ARIA-E was significantly lower (5.4% in non-carriers, 10.9% in heterozygotes), and the clinical efficacy (-0.41 to -0.49 points on CDR-SB) was preserved. CHMP concluded that for this restricted sub-group, the benefit-risk balance is positive.

## 4. Safety Warnings & Risk Management
- **Controlled Access & Controlled Distribution:** European approval requires a strict Risk Management Plan (RMP) including an educational program for clinicians, patient alert cards, and controlled distribution.
- **Serial MRI Monitoring:** Mandatory brain MRIs prior to initiation, and before infusions 5, 7, and 14 to detect subclinical ARIA-E or ARIA-H before serious adverse consequences occur.
- **Divergence vs. FDA:** While the US FDA approved Leqembi for the entire early AD population regardless of ApoE genotype, EMA strictly excluded ApoE ε4 homozygotes from European clinical access.
